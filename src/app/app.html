Diploma thesis · University of Ioannina · 2021

A Deep-Unfolded Recurrent Neural Network for Video Anomaly Detection

The network is built by unfolding a proximal gradient method for ℓ1–ℓ1 minimization. It reconstructs compressively sensed video frames and flags a frame as anomalous when its reconstruction PSNR falls below a threshold.

Diploma thesis, University of Ioannina, September 2021. Advisor: Prof. Lisimachos P. Kondi.
