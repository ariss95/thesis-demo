Diploma thesis · University of Ioannina · 2021

Spotting anomalies in compressed video with a deep-unfolded neural network

A recurrent network reconstructs video frames from compressed measurements. When it can't rebuild a frame well, that frame is flagged as anomalous.

Diploma thesis, University of Ioannina, September 2021. Advisor: Prof. Lisimachos P. Kondi.
